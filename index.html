Who We Are

Empowering Women Through Environmental Action

CleanHealth Waste Women's Cooperative creates dignified employment while solving Uganda's healthcare waste management crisis.

Our Mission

To empower women through dignified employment in environmental services while solving Uganda's healthcare waste management crisis through professional, community-based solutions that protect public health and the environment.

Our Vision

To become Uganda's leading women-owned healthcare waste management cooperative, setting the standard for community-based circular economy solutions across East Africa.

Three-Fold Impact of Improper Waste Management

Environmental Impact

  • Medical waste contaminates landfills (especially Kiteezi)
  • Groundwater and soil contamination
  • Air quality degradation from informal burning

Economic Impact

  • Millions of shillings in valuable recyclables lost
  • Paper, cardboard, plastics buried instead of recovered
  • Missed circular economy opportunities

Regulatory Impact

  • NEMA enforcement increasing pressure on facilities
  • Urgent demand for compliance solutions
  • Most facilities lack expertise to comply
Our Approach

Professional, Community-Based Solutions

A women's cooperative providing healthcare waste sorting, non-hazardous collection, and recycling linkage services.

01

Partnership Development

02

Staff Training

Our founder, a Medical Laboratory Technologist, delivers comprehensive waste segregation training to facility staff.

03

Collection Services

Trained cooperative members conduct scheduled pickups of properly segregated non-hazardous waste with full safety protocols.

04

Recycling Linkage

Materials sorted and sold to established recycling partners, completing the circular economy loop.
